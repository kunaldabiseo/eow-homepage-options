Dr. Todd A. Lefkowitz, MD is an ophthalmologist and expert witness. He reviews records, gives standard-of-care and causation opinions, writes expert reports and testifies at deposition and trial in cataract, LASIK, glaucoma, retina, oculoplastic and vision loss cases, for plaintiff and defense attorneys in every state.
